Agro Advisory Platform

End-to-End Crop Intelligence

The Agro Advisory Platform enables data-driven crop management from sowing to harvest. By combining satellite signals, weather intelligence, field sensors, and AI-guided recommendations, it helps farmers improve yield, reduce water waste, and adapt to climate risk across each farming zone.

Field Intelligence

Satellite imagery and geospatial mapping help identify crop health, field variability, irrigation zones, and risk hotspots before action is taken.

Weather & Climate

Real-time rainfall, temperature, wind, and climate forecasts enable timely sowing, irrigation, and protection decisions.

AI Recommendations

Machine-learning models prioritize irrigation, nutrition, and crop protection based on crop stage and field conditions.

Farmer Guidance

Actionable advice is shared through a clear dashboard to help farmers reduce waste and operate with confidence.

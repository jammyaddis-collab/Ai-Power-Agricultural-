Agro Advisory Platform

End-to-End Crop Intelligence

The Agro Advisory Platform provides a comprehensive, data-driven approach to optimize agricultural practices from sowing to harvesting. By leveraging advanced technologies such as satellite imagery, weather data, and AI-based analytics, this platform supports farmers in making informed decisions at each stage of the crop lifecycle, enhancing productivity, minimizing resource wastage, and ensuring sustainability.

Field Intelligence

Satellite imagery and geospatial field mapping identify crop health, soil variability, irrigation zones, and risk areas before the farmer acts.

Weather & Climate

Real-time rainfall, temperature, wind and climate forecasts allow timely decisions on planting windows, irrigation schedules, and pest risk management.

AI Recommendations

Machine-learning models process crop history, environmental conditions and sensor data to prioritize irrigation, nutrition and crop protection.
